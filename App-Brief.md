## Who is this app for?

Operations analyst who tracks the company's current assets and handles reporting. Works mixed between a desk and a control room, using a desktop or laptop browser.

## What problem does this solve?

Operations analysts find assets by browsing Fusion's full data explorer, which takes too long. As a result, about 2 reports a month are 1 to 3 days late.
